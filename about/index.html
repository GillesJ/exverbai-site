Gilles Jacobs pairs a PhD in NLP with production AI engineering: open benchmarks, tested models, and clear ownership of delivery.

Exverbai is an independent AI engineering practice led by Gilles Jacobs. We build systems that find information, understand documents, and automate repetitive work. From prototype to production, you work directly with an engineer who combines language research with practical delivery, clear milestones, and measurable results.

AI engineering that moves your business forward

Exverbai builds AI software that helps people find answers, make informed decisions, and spend less time on repetitive work. We combine artificial intelligence, data engineering, and software development to build new products and improve the systems your business already relies on.

Our work includes intelligent assistants, document analysis, reporting, and workflow automation. From assessing an idea to deploying a working system, we connect the technology to a clear purpose: useful capabilities for your users, measurable improvements for your organisation, and software your team can maintain.

Research depth. Practical experience.

Exverbai is led by Gilles Jacobs, an engineer with a PhD in natural language processing and more than a decade of experience across AI research and commercial software.

His work spans financial services, insurance, healthcare, life sciences, and customer service. Projects have ranged from interpreting financial news and making organisational knowledge accessible to supporting clinical writing and conversation coaching. That breadth brings a practical perspective: understand the domain, test the assumptions, and judge the technology by how well it serves the people using it.
